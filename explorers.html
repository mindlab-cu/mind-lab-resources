Ways to see what the brain & body are really doing.

MIND Lab builds hands-on, interactive tools that turn neuroscience and biofeedback methodology into something you can explore yourself. Pick a tool below to open it.

Fixations · Saccades · Pupillometry

Eye Tracking Explorer

This explorer teaches the science of gaze: how fixations and saccades are measured, what pupillometry reveals about the mind, how scanpaths and attention heatmaps are built from raw gaze data, how patterns shift with expertise, and the clinical markers that eye movement can surface.

TOPICS — SCANPATHS · ATTENTION HEATMAPS · EXPERTISE PATTERNS · CLINICAL MARKERS
Open the explorer
Camera Physics · Emissivity · Colour Maps

Thermal Imaging Explorer

This explorer teaches infrared thermal imaging: how thermal cameras turn heat into an image, what emissivity means for accurate readings, how colour maps translate temperature into something readable, and how facial heat signatures reflect emotion, wellbeing, and cognition.

TOPICS — CAMERA PHYSICS · EMISSIVITY · COLOUR MAPS · FACIAL HEAT SIGNATURES
Open the explorer
Frequency Bands · ERPs · Oscillations · BCIs

EEG Explorer

A self-contained interactive digital textbook on the brain's electrical activity. Covers recording and cleaning, frequency analysis, event-related potentials, oscillations and connectivity, and real-world applications including sleep staging, seizure detection, brain–computer interfaces, and source localisation — each section paired with an animated panel you can manipulate and an optional maths layer.

TOPICS — ARTIFACT HANDLING · SLEEP STAGING · SEIZURE MARKERS · SOURCE LOCALISATION
