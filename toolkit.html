Teaching Toolkit

Bring the Science of Happiness into your classroom

A growing library of ready-to-run activities, discussion guides, and reflection exercises that teachers can use to introduce well-being concepts — no special equipment or training required.

  • 🙏

    Understanding My Daily Life (Myths vs Facts)

    A hands-on look at how everyday choices shape your happiness.
  • 🧘

    Hedonic Adaptation

    An interactive experiment in why happiness spikes, fades, and quietly returns to baseline.
  • 👉

    Nudge Cards

    A card game where a moment happens to you and nothing appears on its own — you have to reach for your deck and pick the thought or action that fits, in the right order, before the moment passes.
  • 🫂

    Empathy Training

    A training room for the six ways we answer someone's pain, and the one move almost everybody gets wrong.
  • 📏

    Measuring by Instruments and Scales

    Choose the right instrument for your study — measures, filtered by what you're measuring, who's answering, and how much time you have.
  • 🥼

    Build methodology for your Project

    Turn a finalised problem statement into a defensible methodology — and into the steps you then follow to finish the project.
