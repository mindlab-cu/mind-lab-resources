Sampling is where student projects quietly lose their claims. Convenience sampling is acceptable in a semester project — pretending it was random is not.

Your analysis is not a choice. It is a consequence of four things you already decided.

Number of groups, whether the same people appear in every condition, the measurement level of your outcome, and your sample size. Walk the tree; the leaf you land on is your test. This is Prof. Mandal's plan-of-study chart, made walkable.

Name the confounds yourself, before your examiner does.

Quantitative work defends validity; qualitative work defends trustworthiness. They are different vocabularies for the same obligation — showing that your finding is about the world and not about your procedure.

Consent is a conversation you can prove happened.

Voluntary participation, informed consent, debriefing, and sharing results back — the four obligations in the methods lecture — plus data protection, which now does most of the work in practice.
