When the unwritten deal breaks

A social contract is the promise nobody wrote down: effort for recognition, loyalty for security, care for care. It breaks quietly, then loudly. This page does not ask you to do anything. It asks what has already happened, and reads it back as odds.

Stages 1–4 are evidence. Past tense throughout. An unanswered question and a stated intention both count zero — nothing here is a task you are failing.

Stage 5 is the choice. What you answer in stage 2 decides what the later stages ask and which two futures stage 5 compares.

04

What it's worth

Evidence

What this asks Judge the future only. What you have already invested is gone whichever way this goes, so it is not a reason to stay.

1. If this ended, what would you lose?

Count only what is still ahead — income, people, standing, opportunities.

2. What is carrying this costing you?

The ongoing drain of the resentment, not the original incident.

3. If you walked away, what is waiting?

Leaving is only a real option if there is somewhere to go. Otherwise the honest version of disengaging is to stay and withdraw the goodwill.

4. Has this happened before?

The heaviest answer on the page. A one-off can be repaired; a habit can only be managed or left.
